Hey there! My name's Golden, but you can also call me Rose. I'm a Canadian Content Creator who loves to make people laugh. I'm a bit of a jack-of-all-trades - I love taking pictures, playing video games, and even dabbling in Computer Science. But my true passion is helping animals. I've been an animal lover for as long as I can remember, and I want to make a difference in their lives. With my YouTube channel, I try my best to edit and post as many videos as I can. But I'm a busy bee, so I sometimes stream on my Twitch. So, if you're interested in checking out my content, feel free to follow me on Twitch and subscribe to my YouTube channel. I'll be sure to let you know when I'm live on Twitch or posting a new video. Thanks for checking me out!
